# Privacy-policy-for-FrictionLog
Privacy Policy for FrictionLog

Last updated: November 25, 2026

FrictionLog does NOT collect any data from any user.

Data Collection: We collect ZERO data. No name, no email, no phone number, no location, no device ID, no contacts, no photos, no usage analytics, no cookies, no advertising ID.

What the app stores: The app lets you locally save your own entries, such as Activity / Friction Name, Minutes Consumed per Occurrence, Frequency, Category, and Optional Notes. All of this information is stored ONLY on your device.

Data Storage: All your friction logs are stored locally on your device only. We cannot access, see, or delete them. We do not have any server to store your data.

Third-Party Sharing: We do not share any data with any third party, because we do not collect any data.

Internet: The app works fully offline and does not require internet to function. It does not send any data to the internet.

Children's Privacy: As no data is collected from anyone, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy: If this policy changes in the future, we will update this page.

Contact Us: If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: quantexiqpvt@gmail.com
